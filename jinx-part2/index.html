From Powder
to Problem

She was a kid with clever hands and a name nobody feared. Zaun took the kid, kept the hands, and gave back something louder. Now every rooftop in the Undercity knows the sound of her laugh arriving about two seconds before the ceiling does.

She builds what shouldn't work, fires what shouldn't aim, and walks away from what shouldn't have survived. Piltover calls it terrorism. She calls it Tuesday.
